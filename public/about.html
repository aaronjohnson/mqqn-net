About

The Work

I make software that starts with something real and stays true to it.

Step Quest turns your daily steps into progress. Digital Forester identifies species and documents timber for small woodland family owners, developed in collaboration with Epilogue Lumber, people who recover quality sawlogs from trees that deserve better than mulch. High Clearance overlands the Cascades of Skamania County on ground measured by lidar, with hidden radios to hunt. It's coming soon to Steam.

Smaller tools come out of daily use: jantar puts a whole machine on one screen, and misra cross-checks a laptop's sensors with logic rules. The physical projects live at bench.mqqn.net, a bin and a growing file for each.

The Person

mqqn is Aaron Johnson, a software developer who has watched computing evolve from green phosphor terminals to smartphones that know where you are on Earth.

Along the way: seizure prediction algorithms for epilepsy at Flint Hills Scientific, after an M.S. at the University of Kansas; pre-press; dynamic postcards for real estate agents; a metropolitan wireless network startup; an agency; Jama Software; a digital day-and-date printing press at Dark Horse, with comics on sale in the app the same day as in stores; software-defined networks at ZeroTier, as a data engineer and solution architect; and math algorithms and remote lights-out data center operations at Intel.

Closer to home: special ops at personaltelco.net, Free Geek, Rover Scout with the 55th Cascadia, and math tutor for gang-affected youth in Rock Creek.

The domain was registered in the 90s, when three-letter .coms were already gone. It persisted. (The Wayback Machine has the history. For prehistory, there's math.ukans.edu.)

The Approach

No ads. No data harvesting. No dark patterns. Apps should be tools that serve their users, not products that exploit them.

These are small projects, built on my own dime or for friends, because I want them to exist. Some of it happens in the open. Where a model can run on your device, it does. AI helps me write the code, and I answer for what ships.
